When and where to see cranes in Extremadura

How many cranes winter here, when they arrive, the best sites, and how to watch them without disturbing them.

Winter · Oct to MarArticle
Wild Dehesa

Birdwatching in Extremadura, Spain. Please keep your distance from nests in the breeding season, stay on tracks, and listen as much as you look, since many species are found by their calls.

Written from Hoyos, in the Sierra de Gata. Stay with us at Casa Rural Jardín de la Sierra de Gata. Contact: info@wilddehesa.com
