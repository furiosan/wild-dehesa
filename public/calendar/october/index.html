Birdwatching in Extremadura in October

Cranes arrive.

Arrival dates vary by year. Dusk roost flights are the best show.

Griffon Vulture and Spanish Imperial Eagle are easy to see in October. Common Crane and Great Bustard are arriving, leaving or harder to find.

Look for
  • First Common Cranes from northern Europe
  • Autumn colour in the oaks
  • Acorn season in the dehesa
Where to go
  • Borbollón reservoir
  • Alagón valley fields
