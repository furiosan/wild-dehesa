Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-10.

Who we are

Wild Dehesa (wilddehesa.com) is a birdwatching guide run from Hoyos, Cáceres, Spain. Contact: caceresruralhoyos@gmail.com.

What this site collects

This site does not set cookies and does not use third-party trackers. Fonts are served from this site, not from a third party. Our host, Cloudflare, processes visitors' IP addresses to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply and to arrange what you asked for, such as a guide introduction. We keep it as long as the conversation needs it and do not sell or share it for marketing.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you by emailing us. If you are unhappy with how we handle it, you can complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the AEPD.

Links to other sites

This site links to the casa's own site and to official tourism and conservation sources. Their privacy practices are their own.

Wild Dehesa

Birdwatching in Extremadura, Spain. Please keep your distance from nests in the breeding season, stay on tracks, and listen as much as you look, since many species are found by their calls.
