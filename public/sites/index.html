Where to go birdwatching in Extremadura

Sites that cover the region's main habitats. Each guide has what to see, when to go and how to plan the day.

Monfragüe National Park

Cliffs, vultures and nesting Black Storks where the Tagus meets the Tiétar. The best single day in the region.

Cáceres · Mar to MayGuide

Sierra de Gata and Borbollón

Wintering cranes at the reservoir, oak and olive woods, and quiet stone villages on the official route.

Cáceres · Nov to FebGuide

Arrocampo reservoir

Reedbeds beside the Almaraz power station, where warm, steady water draws swamphens, herons and bitterns.

Cáceres · Spring, autumn, winterGuide

Llanos de Cáceres and Trujillo

Wide steppe plains for bustards, larks and raptors, with the old town of Trujillo close by.

La Serena

Treeless grassland that tour operators rank among the best places in the world for steppe birds such as Little Bustard and sandgrouse.
